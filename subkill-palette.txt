========================================================================
 USAGE CHEAT-SHEET
========================================================================
 Primary CTA (Cancel / Kill):  bg-kill-600 hover:bg-kill-700 text-white
 Money saved figure:           text-save-600  (dark: text-save-400)
 Money leaking figure:         text-leak-600  (dark: text-leak-400)
 Renewal-soon badge:           bg-warn-500/10 text-warn-700 border-warn-500/30
 Danger / overdue badge:       bg-leak-500/10 text-leak-600 border-leak-500/30
 Success badge:                bg-save-500/10 text-save-700 border-save-500/30
 Info tip:                     bg-info-500/10 text-info-700 border-info-500/30

 CONTRAST: on dark surfaces use the 300/400 steps for text; on light
 surfaces use 600/700. Never put 500 text on a 500 fill.
