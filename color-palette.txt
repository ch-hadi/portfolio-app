========================================================================
 USAGE TIPS
========================================================================
- On dark backgrounds, use the lighter steps (300/400) for text to keep
  WCAG contrast >= 4.5:1. On light backgrounds, use 600/700.
- Badge pattern:  border-<c>-500/30  bg-<c>-500/10  text-<c>-600  dark:text-<c>-300
- Primary action: bg-brand-600 hover:bg-brand-700 text-white
